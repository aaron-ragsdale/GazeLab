About this experiment

MediaPipe estimates iris and facial landmarks, not true visual fixation. Calibration is specific to your camera, display, lighting and posture. Validate independently before research use; do not interpret gaze as attention. No footage is uploaded by this app.

Before you calibrate

A few quick steps for better gaze estimates.

  1. Sit about 18–24 inches (45–60 cm) from the camera, with your face centered and well lit.
  2. Keep your head comfortably still. Follow each dot using your eyes, not your head.
  3. Look directly at the dot, then press Space without looking at your keyboard or the capture button.
  4. Keep looking at the dot until it moves (about 2.4 seconds). Repeat for each target.

Stay in the same position after calibration. Use the same display and camera setup for recording. Run validation afterward to check accuracy.
